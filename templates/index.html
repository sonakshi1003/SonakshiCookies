A Little About Us

More than just cookies.

Sonakshi's Cookies is a small personal baking business focused on fresh cookies, thoughtful packaging, and giving back through animal-shelter donations. Every box is mixed, scooped, and baked by hand in Bengaluru — no big batches, no shortcuts.

A Note From Sonakshi

I bake because it makes me happy.

This bakery started small — no grand plan, just a love for baking and a soft spot for animals that I could never quite separate from each other.

Every order that comes through this site helps keep both of those things going: another batch of cookies, and a small monthly donation to shelters that could really use it.

I can't promise it makes a huge difference. But even a little difference, in even one animal's life, feels worth trying for.

Sonakshi

Allergy information

Sonakshi's Cookies is not a nut-free, dairy-free, egg-free, gluten-free, or allergen-free kitchen.

Cookies may contain or come into contact with common allergens including wheat, dairy, eggs, soy, and tree nuts. Nutella Stuffed Cookies may contain hazelnuts.

If you have a severe allergy, please contact us before placing an order.
